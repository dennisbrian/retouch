## Research decisions from this review

1. **Use this bundle as a manual compositing reference.** Its strongest
   evidence concerns layer order, masks, scale/depth, edge treatment, and
   scene lighting for stylized cosplay work.
2. **Keep natural face quality unresolved.** The photographed pages do not
   provide registered source/output pairs, full-resolution skin detail, or
   independent judgments of likeness and preservation. A visible intermediate
   step is evidence that the author used a method, not that the method is safe
   or effective in Retouch.
3. **Treat face beautification as an explicit intent.** Image 225's Liquify
   instruction and image 144's painted catchlight call for separate user intent
   and preservation criteria. They are not general face cleanup rules.
4. **Do not turn numeric tutorial values into recipe constants.** The pages
   show old Photoshop dialogs under unknown source and display conditions.
   Even legible values apply to those particular layered composites.
