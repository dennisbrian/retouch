# Natural v2 implementation and initial review

Date: 2026-10-22. Candidate implemented against main at f67541e.
Status: selectable experimental candidate; not the application default.

## Behavior

`natural_v2` inherits `natural`, adds `mark_policy=protect_identity` and
`skin.mole_protect=1.0`, sets `skin.rosy=0`, disables legacy blemish healing
(`skin.blemish=0`), and enables targeted `skin.spot_heal=30` (raw 0-100 units).
Smoothing remains 30. No new reshaping, shadow fill or powder finish is added.
The latter operations are deferred until evidence supports their inclusion.

`skin.blemish` is now an independent recipe key, expressed as a 0-1 ratio.
Engine and GUI both honor explicit zero. If absent, both retain their historical
smoothing-derived behavior. Explicit caller/CLI strengths still override recipes.
The candidate is curated for GUI/CLI access, labelled Experimental / compare first,
and excluded from recommendations. Mark protection remains heuristic.

### Healer comparison after the October 21 legacy changes

Both current healers were invoked on the existing spot-heal test's textured
synthetic light, medium and dark skin, with a planted pimple and mole, at
strengths 30 and 55. No policy/mole-protection mask was supplied, so this compares
the healers themselves, not a fully protected pipeline.

| Strength | Healer | Remaining pimple residual / original | Mole change (local LAB distance) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 30 | Legacy | 0.995-1.000 | 17.548-41.915 |
| 30 | Spot heal | 0.071-0.074 | 0 |
| 55 | Legacy | 0.870-1.000 | 19.404-41.937 |
| 55 | Spot heal | 0.071-0.074 | 0 |

Lower residual means a better match to the clean synthetic image. These six
synthetic configurations support trying spot heal at 30; they do not measure
real acne recall, freckle preservation or performance across real skin tones.
Raw results: `/tmp/retouch-natural-v2-review/healers.json`.

### Real renders and visual inspection

EXIF (FUJIFILM X-T4, camera firmware software tag). It was rendered at native
4160 x 6240 after OpenCV honored EXIF orientation, with no max-dim reduction.

|---|---|---|---|

Visual observation: the candidate shows less added skin brightening; the visible
makeup, lashes, lip outline and facial contours appear intact in the reviewed
crops. This is an initial visual check, not a blinded preference result or an
identity/mark-survival certification. The DSLR sample has two overlapping face
boxes for one subject under both recipes, an existing detection limitation that
can cause repeated processing; it is not evidence of two independently evaluated
people. No detection changes were made here.

## Integration with concurrent main changes

Before pushing, origin/main advanced to fe0b34d with another natural_v2
candidate, identity QA and the Protect/Restore phase. The overlapping recipe was
reconciled to the candidate reviewed in this session: rosy 0, mole protection 1,
shadow fill off. Both sets of natural_v2 tests were retained, along with the
remote engine and GUI features. Duplicate recipe definitions and catalog entries
were removed. The blemish ParamSpec now advertises its real recipe path and units.
395 focused recipe/parameter/cookbook tests passed after conflict resolution.

The renders and 334-test result above describe the initial f67541e-based
implementation. Upstream added skin and detection changes; rerender the integrated
branch before treating those earlier images as its current output. The documented
synthetic healer comparison remains a narrow study, not broad quality acceptance.
